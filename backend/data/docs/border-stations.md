# Border stations and trips abroad

Starter content, checked 2026-10-11. Sources: ticket-rechner.de (updated 2026-07-21), bus-und-bahn.de, de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutschlandticket. Verify before travelling.

## Austria: Salzburg and Kufstein

The Deutschlandticket is valid to two stations in Austria: Salzburg Hbf and Kufstein. You can travel to Salzburg with the ticket as long as you arrive via Freilassing on a regional train (BRB, DB or ÖBB S-Bahn); from München the direct regional train takes about two hours. Kufstein is covered via Kiefersfelden on BRB trains from München or Rosenheim. The ticket is not valid on buses in Salzburg or on onward trains into Austria (for example to Innsbruck or Vienna). Westbahn and Railjet trains on the München–Salzburg line are long-distance trains, so the ticket is not valid on them.

German keywords: Österreich, österreichisch, Tirol, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland, Wien

## Austria: the Außerfern line through Reutte

The Deutschlandticket is also valid on one regional line that runs partly through Austria: the Außerfern line Kempten – Pfronten – Reutte in Tirol – Ehrwald – Garmisch-Partenkirchen. On this line the ticket is valid for through journeys between German stations and for trips starting or ending in Germany. Journeys wholly inside Austria, such as Reutte to Ehrwald, are not covered. The Außerfern line is a scenic way to link the Allgäu with Garmisch.

German keywords: Österreich, Tirol, Außerfernbahn, Durchfahrt, Transit, Allgäu

## Switzerland: Basel and Schaffhausen

The Deutschlandticket is valid to two places in Switzerland: Basel and Schaffhausen. Basel Badischer Bahnhof is a German-operated station on Swiss soil and is covered. Regional trains from Weil am Rhein and Lörrach also continue to Basel SBB. Schaffhausen is covered on the regional trains from Singen and Erzingen, and trains between Singen and Waldshut pass through it. The ticket is not valid on Swiss trams, buses and onward trains into Switzerland (for example to Zürich); they need a Swiss ticket.

German keywords: Schweiz, schweizerisch, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland, Hochrhein

## France: Wissembourg, Lauterbourg and Sarreguemines

The Deutschlandticket is valid to three towns in France just across the border: Wissembourg, Lauterbourg and Sarreguemines. It is not valid to Strasbourg. From the Palatinate, regional trains reach Wissembourg (via Winden and Schweighofen) and Lauterbourg (via Wörth and Berg). From Saarbrücken, the Saarbahn tram-train S1 runs to Sarreguemines (Saargemünd). saarVV buses also reach Creutzwald and Carling. The tram from Kehl to Strasbourg is not covered. In July and August 2026 there was a summer offer for under-28s living in Baden-Württemberg, Rhineland-Palatinate or Saarland covering regional trains in the Grand Est region.

German keywords: Frankreich, französisch, Elsass, Lothringen, Straßburg, Weißenburg, Saargemünd, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland, Pfalz

## Netherlands: Venlo, Arnhem, Enschede and Hengelo

The Deutschlandticket is valid to four stations in the Netherlands: Venlo, Arnhem, Enschede and Hengelo. Venlo is covered via Kaldenkirchen (RE 13 from Düsseldorf and Mönchengladbach). Arnhem Centraal is covered via Emmerich (RE 19 from Düsseldorf). Enschede is covered via Gronau (RB 51 from Dortmund, RB 64 from Münster). Hengelo is covered via Bad Bentheim. Around Aachen, AVV buses reach Vaals and Kerkrade. Dutch trains and buses beyond these points (for example to Amsterdam) need a Dutch ticket.

German keywords: Niederlande, Niederlanden, niederländisch, Holland, Arnheim, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland

## Belgium and Luxembourg

The Deutschlandticket is valid to only one place in Belgium and, under a pilot, on one railway line into Luxembourg. In Belgium only Kelmis is covered, on AVV buses from Aachen; trains to Belgium are not included. For Luxembourg, the regional trains on the Trier – Wasserbillig – Luxembourg line (including CFL trains) and several cross-border bus lines were reported as covered until 31 December 2026 under a pilot. Public transport inside Luxembourg is free for everyone anyway. The Saarbrücken–Luxembourg express bus is not covered.

German keywords: Belgien, belgisch, Luxemburg, Lüttich, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland

## Denmark: Tønder

The Deutschlandticket is valid to one station in Denmark: Tønder, and only on the RB 66 from Niebüll. There is no route from Flensburg into Denmark on which the ticket is valid, and the Puttgarden–Rødby ferry to Denmark is not included.

German keywords: Dänemark, dänisch, Tondern, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland, Fähre

## Poland: Świnoujście and Zgorzelec

The Deutschlandticket is valid to a few stations in Poland right on the border: Świnoujście, Zgorzelec and Krzewina Zgorzelecka. Świnoujście Centrum on Usedom is the last stop of the Usedomer Bäderbahn from Züssow and Heringsdorf and is covered; the beach and town centre are within walking distance. Zgorzelec, across the Neisse from Görlitz, is covered by the regional train from Görlitz, and Krzewina Zgorzelecka is a stop on the Görlitz–Zittau line that lies on the Polish bank. Trains further into Poland, to Szczecin, Wrocław or Poznań, are not covered beyond the last German tariff point.

German keywords: Polen, polnisch, Swinemünde, Stettin, Breslau, Posen, Neiße, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland

## Czech Republic: Hrádek, Varnsdorf and Vejprty

The Deutschlandticket is valid to three stations in the Czech Republic: Hrádek nad Nisou, Varnsdorf and Vejprty. Trilex trains from Zittau pass through Hrádek nad Nisou and Varnsdorf on their way to Liberec and Seifhennersdorf; the ticket is valid to those two Czech stations. Vejprty is covered from Bärenstein in the Ore Mountains. Journeys further into the Czech Republic, to Děčín, Ústí or Prague, need a Czech ticket from the border.

German keywords: Tschechien, tschechisch, Tschechische Republik, Prag, Reichenberg, Weipert, Erzgebirge, Böhmen, Grenzbahnhof, Ausland
